About Me 个人简介

Currently an assistant professor at Shanghai University. Obtained Ph.D. in Computer Science from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (2024) under Prof. Liyao Xiang (向立瑶). Obtained M.S. in Mathematics from Nanjing University of Science and Technology (2019). Research focuses on differential privacy, adversarial learning, and federated learning.
